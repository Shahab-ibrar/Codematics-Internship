RED CARD 2018

China Digital Football Awards

The Red Card is globally recognised as the leading annual report to assess the most influential European football clubs, leagues and players online in China. Now in its 7th year, Red Card 2018 has been reimagined to include 5 additional awards to recognise areas of digital performance in China, thereby taking the Red Card level to a new level. Due to the nature and variety of these new categories, the winners this year will be chosen by a panel of 6 industry experts, rather than by the traditional metrics used to assess the three headline awards.
